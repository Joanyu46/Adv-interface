Planner

A planner list set like an open-call poster: black, hairline outlines, a classic display serif, and one calendar pastel per day. Date rows are D-shaped bars, the rest are pills; hovering morphs a row round and opens it into ( ) brackets. Expanding a day or the reminders closes the row into a circle, reopens it, and unfolds a second outline underneath with its tasks.
