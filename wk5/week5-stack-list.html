Advanced Interface · Week 5 · List

Stack List

A list item built like a stack of frosted cards. Hovering carves a notch out of the corner, the white action pinches off the glass like a drop of liquid and falls into it, and a soft light follows your pointer across the glass. Tapping a row dissolves the list and brings the next level out of a blur, and expanding a row ripples a glass sub-card open from under the button.
