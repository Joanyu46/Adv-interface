Pearl Search

Tone: Ethereal. A party filter row: a pearl-rimmed search orb and two pop-up pills. Searching stretches the orb across the row while the pills drift away. Closing pulls the bar back like liquid: it shrinks, squashes, wobbles into a circle and a pearl light sweeps through it. In each dropdown a light purple glass highlight moves like a drop of liquid — it stretches toward the row you point at and pulls its tail in after — while an icon reel rolls to the matching glyph. Picking squishes the drop, sends the icon off to the right, draws a check and sweeps a light through it before the menu folds up.
