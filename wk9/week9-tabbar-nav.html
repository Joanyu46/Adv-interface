01 · Tab bar (horizontal) + sub-navigation

Tab bar · icon boxes + sub-navigationThe selected tab lifts into a glass box and shows its label. Its pages fold out below with category headers, a right slot (badge, text or icon) and a second line on the active page. ←→ tabs · ↑↓ pages
